Machine Learning Analysis & Model Evaluation

Detailed breakdown of data preprocessing, feature engineering, optimal K-selection metrics, PCA dimensionality reduction, and cluster characteristics derived from SmartCart.ipynb.

Data Preprocessing & Cleaning

  • Missing Value Imputation: 24 missing Income entries filled with median income.
  • Feature Engineering: Calculated Age (2026 - Year_Birth), Customer_Tenure_Days, Total_spending (sum of 6 product categories), and Total_children.
  • Categorical Mapping: Standardized Education (Graduate, Postgraduate, Undergraduate) and Living_With (Partner vs. Alone).
  • Outlier Removal: Filtered out extreme outliers (Age ≥ 90 and Income ≥ $600,000), reducing dataset from 2,240 to 2,236 clean customer samples.
  • Scaling & OHE: One-Hot Encoded categorical variables and standardized all 18 features via StandardScaler.

Optimal Cluster Selection (K = 4)

To determine the optimal number of segments, both the Elbow Method (within-cluster sum of squares) and Silhouette Analysis were evaluated on the 3D PCA projection space.

KneeLocator Elbow Point: K = 4
Clustering Algorithm: Agglomerative (Ward Linkage)
PCA Components: 3 Components (~44.96% Variance)
