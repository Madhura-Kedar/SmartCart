SMARTCART
E-Commerce Customer Segmentation

An unsupervised machine learning system designed to discover hidden consumer personas from customer demographic, purchasing, and channel engagement data.

Explore Analysis Try Segmentation

Project Overview

The Problem

E-commerce retail platforms collect massive raw customer logs, but struggle to extract actionable insights. Generic, one-size-fits-all marketing wastes ad spend on unaligned customer demographics.

The Objective

Group customers into distinct, high-value behavioral segments using unsupervised ML algorithms, allowing marketing teams to deliver personalized campaigns and optimize product recommendations.

What SmartCart Does

Processes customer records, cleans missing values and outliers, engineers total spending & household attributes, applies 3D PCA, and categorizes customers into 4 distinct personas using Agglomerative Clustering.

Machine Learning Workflow

STEP 1
Raw Data Ingestion
smartcart_customers.csv (2,240 records, 22 columns)
STEP 2
Preprocessing & Cleaning
Median Income Imputation + Outlier Filter (Age < 90, Income < $600k)
STEP 3
Feature Engineering
Created Age, Tenure, Total Spending, Total Children & Grouped Categories
STEP 4
Standard Scaling & OHE
One-Hot Encoded Categoricals + StandardScaler across 18 Features
STEP 5
PCA Dimensionality Reduction
PCA(n_components=3) for 3D Spatial Vector Projection
STEP 6
Agglomerative Clustering (K=4)
Ward Linkage Clustering validated by Elbow Method & Silhouette Score
OUTPUT
4 Customer Personas
Actionable Customer Segments for Targeted E-Commerce Campaigns
